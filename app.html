The app

Your data, turned into one next move.

Drop in your wearable export. In a second or two you get a real healthspan dashboard, a prioritized plan, and the single highest-leverage move for today, all computed on this device. Not a scripted demo. Your actual numbers.

Your file never leaves this device. It is read in your browser with the local file API, analyzed in memory, and forgotten when you reload. No upload, no account, no server, no storage. You can turn off wifi and it still works. Identity files in the export (name, email) are ignored on purpose.

the .zip you downloaded, or the .csv files inside it. Or click to choose.

How to get your Withings export
  1. Sign in at account.withings.com on a computer.
  2. Open Download my data in your profile settings.
  3. Request the export, then download the .zip when it is ready.
  4. Come back here and drop the .zip in the box above.

Oura, Apple Health, and Google Health support are next. Today the app reads Withings Health Mate exports.
