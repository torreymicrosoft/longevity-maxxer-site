Our method

Do less, better.

The longevity space rewards buying more: more devices, more supplements, more protocols. We take the opposite position. Here is how we decide what is worth your attention, and what is not.

First principle

Healthspan, not just lifespan.

Lifespan is how long you live. Healthspan is how long you live well, with energy, strength, and independence. The gap between the two is often a decade or more of decline, and it is the part most worth fighting for.

So we optimize for the years that feel good, not a number on a chart. Almost everything that extends healthspan is unglamorous and free: moving, lifting, sleeping, eating well, staying connected, and keeping up with basic care.

Lifespan

Total years lived. Important, but it says nothing about the quality of those years.

Healthspan

Years lived in good function and vitality. This is what our nine pillars are built to protect.

How we rank

Leverage, times evidence, times your gap.

Every suggestion is ordered by three things. It is a simple idea that keeps effort where it pays off.

In plain terms: a habit rises to the top of your plan when it matters a lot, is well supported by evidence, and is an area where you have real room to improve. That is why two people can answer the same questions and get different first steps.

Honest labeling

What our evidence tiers mean.

We grade claims so you can see the strength of the science, not just a confident sentence.

Strong evidence Moderate evidence Emerging evidence

Strong

Backed by large, consistent studies or broad clinical consensus. Safe to build your foundation on.

Moderate

Good support from observational and mechanistic evidence, though details are still debated. Reasonable to adopt.

Emerging

Promising but early or mixed. Fine to explore once the fundamentals are handled, easy to over-invest in.
