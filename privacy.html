Privacy at Longevity Maxxer

Last updated: January 2026

The short version: This website does not collect your personal health information. There is no account to create and no health-data form to fill in. The interactive self-check runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to us.

1. Our privacy-first design

Longevity Maxxer is delivered as a static website. That is a deliberate safety choice. Because there is no application backend that receives your inputs, there is no health profile for us to store, analyze, breach, or sell. We built the product so that privacy is a property of the architecture, not just a policy.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and this site does not transmit to us, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address.
  • Any health information, biometric readings, lab results, or wearable data.
  • Any account credentials, because there are no accounts.

3. The self-check runs on your device

The interactive self-check calculates a general focus score using only the values you set with the on-screen controls. That calculation happens locally in your web browser using client-side code. The values you choose are not sent to a server, are not written to persistent storage, and are gone as soon as you close or refresh the page.

4. Hosting and technical logs

The site is served as static files by a cloud hosting provider. As is standard for any website, the hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and protect the service. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with any health information, because we never receive any.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not sell data to advertisers. If we ever introduce privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand general traffic patterns, we will update this page first and keep any such measurement free of health information.

6. Links to other services

Our pages may reference third-party resources or research. Once you leave this site, the privacy practices of those services apply. We encourage you to review their policies.

7. Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this notice

If our practices change, we will revise this page and update the date above. Because the site is versioned and iterated through code commits, changes are transparent and traceable.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised through the project repository that hosts this site. Please do not send us health information; we are not set up to receive it and do not want it.

This notice describes privacy practices only. It is not legal advice. Longevity Maxxer provides general wellness education and is not a substitute for professional medical care.
