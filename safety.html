Clear boundaries

What Longevity Maxxer is, and is not.

We are proud of what we do not do. A tool that knows its limits is a safer tool.

What it is

  • An educational guide to healthy-living fundamentals.
  • A way to focus on the few habits with the strongest evidence.
  • A private, on-device self-reflection tool.
  • A prompt to show up prepared for your own clinician.

What it is not

  • Not a medical device, and not a diagnosis.
  • Not a prescription, dose, or treatment plan.
  • Not a replacement for your doctor or therapist.
  • Not a place that stores or sells your health data.
In an emergency, do not use this site. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing, or thoughts of harming yourself, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States) or go to the nearest emergency department right away.
